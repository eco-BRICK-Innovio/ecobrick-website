Reference scenario and first-stage fundraising

The baseline models five machines; sites can expand to 10, 15 or more, with assumptions and costs appropriate to their configuration. Site costs weigh heavily on a one-machine operation; under the model assumptions, profitability improves materially at three machines. The $2 million first stage, within a $10 million target, advances the Haifa, Hiriya and Ashdod facilities and company-team growth. It is not the budget for this reference scenario. Cold asphalt · product economics

A separate product scenario. All operating amounts below exclude VAT. Waste gate-fee income is excluded from this conservative calculation.

The production estimate includes materials, binder, intermediate-product manufacture, production wages, electricity and packaging. These costs are not added again. The remaining amount must cover site management and other expenses; it is not EBITDA or net profit.

Equipment, capacity and delivery

One mixing and packaging system: estimated equipment investment ILS 300,000, shown separately from per-tonne operating cost. Equipment VAT and installation scope remain to be confirmed. Planned capacity: 4 finished tonnes per hour. The monthly ceiling uses working hours, working days and utilization selected in this scenario; actual output also depends on intermediate-material supply. No recipe or supply conversion is modeled here.

Whole-pallet sales only. Delivery is free to the customer for two or more pallets between Gedera and Hadera; one pallet or delivery outside that area is charged separately. The scenario conservatively allocates the truck cost across its load without adding customer delivery charges.

This scenario does not change the baseline forecast, staffing, investment or cash-flow calculations below.

50 / 50 · sales product mix

Equal shares by finished-product weight. Operating amounts exclude VAT. Gate-fee income is excluded. This separate scenario does not update the baseline forecast below.

Remaining amounts are before site management and other expenses, depreciation and tax; they are not EBITDA or net profit. Aggregate production, binder, grinding and packaging are included in the unit-cost estimates and are not charged again. Hot-additive delivery is paid by the customer; no delivery margin is assumed.

Equipment and capacity

One cold mixing and packaging system: estimated ILS 300,000; equipment VAT and installation scope remain unconfirmed. One hot-additive grinder including bulk-bag filling: estimated ILS 220,000 excluding VAT. These investments are separate from unit production costs and are not added to the baseline investment below.

Cold-system capacity: 4 finished tonnes/hour. Hot grinder capacity: 2 tonnes/hour. Each product is checked against its own system ceiling. Actual production also depends on aggregate supply and packaging performance; this calculation is not a sales forecast. Hot additive is packed in one-tonne bulk bags with an inner nylon liner.

30 / 70 · sales product mix

30% cold asphalt and 70% aggregate for preparing concrete mixes for blocks, by finished-product weight. Operating amounts exclude VAT. Gate-fee income is excluded. This separate scenario does not update the baseline forecast below.

Remaining amounts are before site expenses, depreciation and tax; they are not EBITDA or net profit. Aggregate production includes binder. The customer pays separately for aggregate bulk bags and delivery, including loose-bulk truck shipments; no packaging or delivery margin is assumed.

Equipment, supply and delivery

One cold mixing and packaging system: estimated ILS 300,000, separate from unit costs; equipment VAT and installation scope remain unconfirmed. Cold-system capacity is 4 finished tonnes/hour. The capacity check covers cold mixing only; aggregate supply for both products must be confirmed separately. No overall site capacity or recipe conversion is calculated.

Concrete-mix aggregate may be supplied in bulk bags or loose in a truck body, including a 32 m³ bulk shipment. The sales price is per cubic metre, not per tonne or per finished block. Quantity in cubic metres is calculated from product tonnes and the selected density. These inputs do not change the baseline forecast or equipment investment below.
