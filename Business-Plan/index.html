Reference scenario and first-stage fundraising

The baseline models five machines; sites can expand to 10, 15 or more, with assumptions and costs appropriate to their configuration. Site costs weigh heavily on a one-machine operation; under the model assumptions, profitability improves materially at three machines. The $2 million first stage, within a $10 million target, advances the Haifa, Hiriya and Ashdod facilities and company-team growth. It is not the budget for this reference scenario.
